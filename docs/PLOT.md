# Design decisions added in chat (after the plot above)

- Combat is real-time, not turn-based. Foes move and attack actively and counter the player's habits. Combos exist but cannot be spammed (the engine enforces this: stale-move damage decay, committed finisher, guard/armor counters).
- Controls stay simple: melee, heavy swing, dash, double jump, damaging roll.
- Hero is a humanoid shadow (Hollow Knight mood, Alan Becker stickman animation feel). 2D side view; occasionally platforms lead into ground/sky layers.
- Areas: the ruins (palace in ruins), a ruined molten-lava mountain with caves (Noctron-like), a grand return palace inspired by the Ultramarines, a modest home village with Japanese-style roads and schools, and the butterfly scene on the school grounds/stadium.
- The game records what the player did for the endings.
- THE MIRROR ("The Hero?") BOSS: fights using the player's own habits from the last 5 boss fights. It is empathic, not brutal. A conscience voice asks: if this mirror is what we were to the other bosses (citizens of the ruined kingdom, mutated, only defending their home), how did we become so cold? Where is our judgment?
- On every other boss, dying respawns at the last checkpoint with a normal death line. ONLY on the Mirror, the death screen says: "How did I become this?"
